How Kerja works

Here’s a morning on Kerja, from opening the app to saying hi.

  • Profile
  • People
  • Cafés
  • Sessions
  • Connect
  • Control

Chapter 1

Show what you’re building

Your profile tells people what you do and what you need. It’s enough to start a good conversation.

Also here

  • Sign in with Apple, Google or your email.
  • One photo, your name and a headline.
  • What you do, and what you’re looking for: content creation, a co-founder, focus buddies…
  • What you’re building, and what you need right now.
  • Up to 3 things you can help with.
  • Your answers to two questions, like “A project I’m proud of…”
  • Open to: coffee, co-working, collabs.
  • Your portfolio and Instagram.
  • How long you’re in Canggu, or that you live there.
  • Your next city, shown only to your connections.

One photo. No age. No follower count.

Chapter 2

Meet the right people today

Every day, Kerja picks three people for you to meet, each with a reason.

You both want focus buddies

Also here

  • For you: 3 people a day, each with a reason, worked out on your phone.
  • Who’s working at cafés, in sessions and around each area right now.
  • Content creators: photographers, videographers, models and editors.
  • Search by name, role or skill.
  • On a profile: what you have in common, and how many times you’ve worked together.
  • A suggested first line, a question about the member’s work, that you can change.

Chapter 3

Find a café that works

The map shows Canggu’s cafés and who’s working there now.

Also here

  • Each café’s hours, and how far it is from you.
  • Wi-Fi and sockets, from members who worked there.
  • The faces of who’s working there now. Someone who hides from “Who’s here” shows as a blank circle, never named.
  • Work here: start working at a café, alone or open to others.
  • The check that you’re at the café happens on your phone, within about 300 metres.
  • Around an area today: Batu Bolong, Canggu, Berawa or Pererenan, and what you’re up for (coffee, co-work or collab).

Chapter 4

Host or join a session

A session is a table at a café, with a time, a few seats and simple rules.

  1. 8:40Running late? Say so.
  2. 8:55I’m here.
  3. 9:00Quiet time.
  4. 10:30Coffee break.
  5. 12:00Check out.

Also here

  • Host a session: Focus, Create content, Brainstorm, Skill swap or Founders’ coffee.
  • Choose the time, the seats and “What we’ll work on”. Add a quiet time and a break from the session’s page.
  • Join with one tap, or “Ask to join” if the host chooses who joins.
  • Full? Join the waitlist. When a seat opens, it’s held for you for 15 minutes.
  • A reminder 15 minutes before the start.
  • Running late or Can’t make it: the host is told, nothing more.
  • Tap I’m here when you arrive, and check out when you leave.
  • Two quick questions afterwards: How was the Wi-Fi? Sockets?

Chapter 5

Say hi and keep in touch

Liked working next to someone? Say hi.

Also here

  • Say hi with a short note, and suggest a café with “Coffee here?”
  • Accept, or Not now. The sender is never told.
  • An Inbox and a chat with each of your connections.
  • “Sent” and “Delivered” under your latest message. “Read” only if you both turn it on.
  • No typing indicator, no online status.
  • Notifications, if you want them: new requests, messages, someone joining or asking to join your session, a session starting soon, a seat opening up. They never show what people write.
  • Me: your month (time worked, sessions, cafés and people met), your weekly streak and your sessions.

Chapter 6

You’re in control

You decide who sees you, and who can reach you.

Also here

  • Hide which café you’re at.
  • Hide from “Who’s here”: you’re counted, never named.
  • Choose who can send you requests: everyone, people you’ve worked with, or nobody new.
  • Share your next city, or don’t.
  • Read receipts on or off.
  • Report and Block on every profile and in chat.
  • Delete your account any time, in the app.
