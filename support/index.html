Get Kerja

Help and support

Write to us at hello@trykerja.com. A person reads every email.

Report someone

In the app, open their profile or your chat with them, tap •••, then Report. You can also press and hold a message to report it. A person reviews every report within 24 hours. In an emergency, call 112 in Indonesia.

Block someone

Tap ••• on their profile or in your chat, then Block. You both stop seeing each other at once.

Delete your account

Me → Settings → Delete account. Your account goes at once. If you signed in with Apple, you sign in with Apple once more first. The privacy policy says what's kept for safety.

Signing in

Kerja has no passwords. Sign in with Apple, Google, or a one-time code sent to your email. A code works for one hour. We never ask anyone for a code.

Working at a café

When you tap Work here or I'm here, your phone checks that you're within about 300 metres of the café. Your position is never sent. If it says you're too far, turn on Precise Location for Kerja in your phone's settings.

Notifications

Turn them on in Me → Settings → Notifications. Nothing arriving? Check that notifications for Kerja are on in your phone's settings.
